# Promotion Insights

## Key findings
- Employees who left had a **shorter** average time since last promotion (1.95 years) than those who stayed (2.23 years).
- By stagnation level, attrition is 17.0% (low: under 3 years), 12.4% (medium: 3 to 4 years) and 14.2% (high: over 4 years). There is no clear upward pattern.

## Business interpretation
In this dataset there is no evidence that a long gap since the last promotion increases attrition. The hypothesis was tested and not supported.

## Recommendations
- Do not prioritise promotion timing as a retention lever based on this data.
- Test the same question on real company data, where promotion cycles and job levels can be compared properly.
