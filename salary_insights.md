# Salary Insights

## Key findings
- Attrition is **25.3%** in the low salary band (below 4,000 per month, 542 employees) against **11.0%** in the medium band and **10.5%** in the high band.
- The average risk score is also highest in the low band (0.43) and lowest in the high band (0.29).
- Above the low band, higher pay makes little difference to attrition (11.0% vs 10.5%).

## Business interpretation
Low pay is where attrition concentrates. Beyond a certain level, extra pay does not seem to reduce attrition much, so other factors (workload, tenure, satisfaction) matter more there.

## Business impact
A targeted review of the lowest-paid group is likely to be more effective than a general pay increase.

## Recommendations
- Benchmark pay for roles in the low band against the market.
- Combine any pay review with workload and engagement actions.

## Caution
Salary bands use fixed cut-offs (4,000 and 8,000) and do not account for job level. A real analysis would compare pay within the same job level.
